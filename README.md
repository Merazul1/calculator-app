## 🧮 Available High-Precision Online Calculators

All tools run client-side in the browser with zero server latency and zero personal data retention:

* **[Annual to Gross & Net Salary Calculator](https://smartcalclab.com/salary):** Compute net take-home paycheck from gross wages. Features regional country presets for the US (FICA + federal tax), UK (PAYE + NI), Spain (IRPF + 14 pagas), Canada (CPP/EI), and India (New Tax Regime + PF), plus popular 2,080 working-hour benchmark tables.
* **[Mortgage Cost Estimator & Loan Calculator](https://smartcalclab.com/loan):** Calculate monthly principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance (PITI), and private mortgage insurance (PMI) with full yearly amortization schedule.
* **[Auto Loan & Car Payment Calculator](https://smartcalclab.com/autoloan):** Estimate vehicle financing payments, trade-in value, sales tax, APR interest rates, and loan amortization over 24 to 84 months.
* **[BMI & Body Health Calculator](https://smartcalclab.com/bmi):** Compute Body Mass Index in metric (cm & kg) or imperial (ft & lbs) with official World Health Organization (WHO) category brackets.
* **[Compound Interest & Wealth Simulator](https://smartcalclab.com/compound):** Forecast exponential investment growth, annual returns, and monthly deposits.
* **[Live Currency Exchange Converter](https://smartcalclab.com/currency):** Mid-market foreign exchange rates across USD, EUR, GBP, MXN, SAR, AED, INR, and global currencies.
* **[Percentage & Discount Calculator](https://smartcalclab.com/percentage):** Solve percentage increases, retail sale markdowns, and sales tax.

## ⚡ Technical Architecture & Performance Highlights

- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 8
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 + Dark Mode persistence
- **Performance:** Ahrefs Site Audit **100/100 Health Score**, sub-200ms Time to First Byte (TTFB)
- **SEO & Schema:** Dynamic Schema.org (`SoftwareApplication`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`)
- **Privacy:** Strict client-side computation with zero telemetry on financial inputs
- **Embeddable:** Free embed widget generation for personal finance blogs and educational sites
